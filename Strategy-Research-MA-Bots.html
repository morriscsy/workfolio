Research summary

Which MA bot to trust

5-minute Mid TP34 is the result to trust.

5-minute 34% take profit 22 of 32 above zero

Seven long-only dip-and-reclaim scripts around MA150 and MA200, on 32 symbols. 1-minute and 5-minute totals from the same workbook. Every figure below was counted again from the data sheet.

00How this analysis was done

Read it in this order. Each step answers one question, and the later steps do not replace the earlier ones.

1
Name what actually changedMarket TP30 is the base. The other six are that bot with a switch flipped. If two columns are the same trade, they are not two ideas.
 2
Score 1-minute and 5-minute, and do not rank by the biggest averageThirty-two symbols. The highest average can be two names. The median and the win rate decide which column to trust.
 3
Separate the timeframe from the scriptTwo strategies lose on the 1-minute average and win on the 5-minute average. Nothing flips the other way.
 4
Split 2X ETFs from stocksFifteen names in each group. A result that works on stocks can lose on the leveraged list.
 5
Check the three midpoint scripts on 1-minute aloneMid TP30, Mid 15m, and Mid TP34. This is where the 34% target does not help the typical symbol.
 6
Ask whether volatility explains it1-minute and 5-minute ATR, as a percent of price. A 2% stop is a few bars on a wild 2X name and many bars on a quiet stock.
 7
Ask what kind of tape the bot wantsDaily path from 23 Mar to 2 Oct. A dip that is kept, not one-day momentum, and not a bounce that is given back.
 8
Keep, drop, or testThe names that are negative in every cell, and the one column to trust first.

Worst strategy cell

What did not flip

Market TP30 and Market MA150 lose on the 1-minute average and win on the 5-minute average. Nothing flips the other way. Every strategy’s average is higher on 5-minute bars.

Comparisons are directional. Each symbol uses its own available bars through 2026-10-03, so the windows are not the same length. Blanks on Market MA150 for CLmain and MNQmain are failed calculations, not zeros. They are excluded. That column is 30 symbols. The other six are 32.

  2. A 15-minute bar only confirms the profit target. It does not close a trade because 15 minutes passed.
  3. Read the median next to the average. Mid Pyramid’s average is MSTR and SMCI. TP34% still leads after those two are removed.

04How the seven differ

Market TP30 is the base. Every other script is that bot with one or two switches flipped. Read down a branch. A grey chip is unchanged. A coloured chip is the change.

Market TP30

Base

Market buy when price reclaims the MA150 / MA200 band. Stop is 2% under the fill. Target is +30% on the chart’s own bar. One entry. No early exit.

Branch A · still a market buy

From the base

Market MA150. Same market buy and same +30% target. Two adds: a flip path that can buy when the band was pointing down, and an exit on an MA150 touch as soon as the trade is green. That early exit is why this one underperforms.

Limit MA23, one step further. Same signals as Market MA150, including the flip path, but the order is a limit at the midpoint of MA150 and MA200. The MA150 exit is blocked until the trade is up at least 23%.

Branch B · limit at the midpoint

From the base

Mid TP30. Drops the flip path and the MA150 exit. The only change from the base is the order: a limit at the midpoint of MA150 and MA200, cancelled if it is still open after 5 bars. Stop and +30% target stay on the chart bar.

Mid 15m. Same as Mid TP30. The +30% target fills only after a closed 15-minute bar has traded through it. The 15-minute bar does not close the trade by itself.

Mid TP34. Same as Mid 15m, plus an on-chart stop and target panel that does not change the orders. The script still defaults to 30%. This backtest used 34%.

Mid Pyramid. Same as Mid 15m, but a new signal can add while a trade is open, up to 3 entries. Every add uses the first entry’s 2% stop, not a fresh stop under its own fill. Each entry still targets +30%, confirmed on 15 minutes. This one was not rerun at 34%.

11What kind of tape this bot wants

Daily closes from 23 Mar 2026 through 2 Oct 2026, the same stretch as the 5-minute tests. A winner here means above zero on every filled 5-minute cell. A loser means below zero on every one. These bars cannot see the 5-minute fill. They only describe the daily path. SPCX and SKHY listed mid-window, so they are left out of the character call.

One-day momentum is not the split. The correlation of today’s return with yesterday’s is near zero on almost every name, winners included. OKLO and OKLL are the exception, at -0.23, so yesterday’s move tended to reverse.

The tape that fits a long-only dip-and-reclaim with a 30% target is a low close early, a later close far above that low, and an ending close that keeps a good part of the gain. INTW, SNDK, INTC, and SMCI did that. The window low was 30 Mar on all four. From that low the later peak was INTW +824.66% (5.6675 to 52.405), SNDK +307.86%, INTC +242.17%, and SMCI +138.22%. On 2 Oct they were still far above the 23 Mar close, and the close sat above the 20-day average on most days. They still had deep drawdowns from a peak, so the path was a trend with holes, not a straight line.

MSTR is only the hole and the recovery. The close went from 82.31 on 26 Jun to 168.50 on 21 Sep, and it finished +15.78% versus 23 Mar. It had more down days than up days, 59 against 75.

The consistent losers are the other shape. The window finished down, down days outnumbered up days, and the close sat under the 20-day and 50-day averages on most days. OKLL, NFXL, and NFLX ended at the low close of the window. A bounce was not the difference. OKLL still ran +161.67% from a March close to a May close, and every 5-minute cell stayed negative, because the higher close was not kept. NFLX never produced a 30% close-to-close continuation at all. The largest was +22.38%.

This is not every winner. CRWG and TSLL won the 5-minute cells and finished the daily window down, -30.40% and -24.32%, after large runs that were given back. GOOG won with a 2.4% average daily range and only 9 days that moved more than 4%. A 2X ETF’s daily range is about twice its underlying: INTC 6.0% against INTW 11.7%, TSLA 3.7% against TSLL 7.3%, NFLX 2.7% against NFXL 5.3%.
